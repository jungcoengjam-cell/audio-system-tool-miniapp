# Audio wiring solder-side photos

The six connector cutouts shipped under `pages/audio-wiring/assets/solder/` were produced with the built-in ImageGen editor from the reference photos below. The edits remove backgrounds and old annotations; small areas obscured by markings may be reconstructed to match nearby metal. These are processed photographic references, not untouched source pixels or precision mechanical drawings. Contact coordinates are visual anchors in the edited image and must not be read as dimensions or manufacturing tolerances.

Earlier generated candidates and prompts remain in `tests/audio-wiring-refactor-2026-10-13/photo-plan/generated/`; the TS replacement prompt is linked above. Production PNGs were reduced to 384 × 384 using numerical crop/resampling with transparency. At display time, the TS view crops to its solder contacts only; the RCA view crops to the center cup and shell clamp, omitting the front insertion pin. The terminal-block photo is a connector-body reference only: the `+ / − / G` function labels are shown left-to-right over the reference image as a schematic order, not as measured hole or screw positions. Confirm each function against the equipment silkscreen before wiring.

For the TS replacement, built-in ImageGen processed the linked Switchcraft SW380 reference to isolate the threaded base, insulator, and two solder lugs. Its 1254 × 1254 candidate was uniformly numerically resampled to 384 × 384 without manual pixel recoloring. The display crop maps source region `(550,255)..(1254,925)` into the production image; the visual T and S anchors are near candidate pixels `(1023,415)` and `(1140,705)`. These are visual contact anchors, not measured manufacturer dimensions.
